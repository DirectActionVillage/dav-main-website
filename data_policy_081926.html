Data Policy

Last updated August 19, 2026

This is a description of what data our website and forms collect, who sees that data, and what eventually happens to that data. We don't have any lawyers, so this isn't a legal privacy policy. If something looks wrong, tell us and we'll try to fix it!

Our website

Our website doesn't run any JavaScript and doesn't create or store any cookies. Verify this yourself with right-click + Inspect or with uBlock Origin.

Our mailing list

We only collect a name and an email address. Any name will do; it doesn't have to be your real legal name. Any email address will do, but we prefer a relatively anonymized address (ideally not firstname.lastname@provider.com) and that you use a privacy-respecting provider like Tuta, RiseUp, or Proton. If all you have is firstname.lastname@gmail.com, we'll still work with it and do our best to protect you, just be aware that some things will be out of our control at that point (like data leakage to your provider). We also don't store the IP address you signed up from. The newsletter software is set not to record it, and the machine serving the form writes no access logs. Our newsletter emails have no tracking, so we don't log opens, clicks, tracking pixels, etc.

Grant applications

There is no grant form yet. Nothing can be applied for today, so nothing is stored or deleted. Here is how it will work when we turn it on.

The questions about your project proposal will be required. Everything about you will be optional: name, email, phone, and Signal username. You will be able to apply with no contact details at all (we just won't be able to tell you if you win)! Grant applications will be deleted 7 days after we announce that cycle's decisions by default, and sooner if you ask.

Where your data lives

The mailing list lives (at-rest) on machines we own and manage (on-prem, not a cloud service), and so do our backups. The disks are encrypted, so they're unreadable if someone walks off with them. One machine we rent does hold your email address for a while, and the next section says when and why. When the grant form exists it will use a separate database, so the part that sends the newsletter won't be able to read applications.

A copy of your records sits in a backup until that backup gets refreshed. So even if you ask us to delete your data, deletion won't be instant. For recovery we keep the last 7 daily backups and the last 4 weekly ones. That's a rolling window, so there's no single number we can promise you. In practice the oldest copy is around a month old, and at worst about six weeks. On 22 September 2026 the oldest was 30 days.

Who sees your data

We fully local-host and send out our own newsletter! We don't use any company or third-party service to deliver or route the mail. Two machines in that path aren't ours.

GitHub serves this website, including the page you're reading. GitHub is a USA company and holds the certificate for directactionvillage.org, so it can see which pages you look at here. It never sees your email address: this website has no form on it. The signup form lives at news.directactionvillage.org, which GitHub does not serve.

A server we rent, in a non-USA country that respects GDPR, handles the mailing list. That rented server:

  • Holds the certificate for news.directactionvillage.org (so it sees what you type into the signup form before passing it on to our on-prem machines).
  • Sends out our newsletter (so it sees your email address if you're on the mailing list).
  • Holds failure notices if emails can't be delivered to you for whatever reason (until our on-prem machines collect the notices, usually within minutes). Those notices also would contain your email address.

We rent a second server in the same country. It isn't in the path above: it never touches your email address, the newsletter, or anything you type into a form.

Leaving, unsubscribing, and data deletion

Every newsletter has a one-click unsubscribe link. That same link opens a page where you can see what we hold, download it, and delete it yourself (without needing to ask anyone). If you would rather talk to a person, email us at directactionvillage@proton.me. As mentioned above, when the grant form exists, applications will be deleted 7 days after we announce that cycle's decisions by default. We can delete them sooner if you ask.

Stuff we can and can't prove to you

The source for this website is public and you can read it on our GitHub. The code that handles your mailing list data is published there too. Realistically you still won't be able to check that the published code is the same code actually running on our machines. Nobody who hosts centralized online services for you can prove that! We're asking you to trust us, but also being honest that nothing we've said above or shown you is a guarantee. At the end of the day, you still need to make your own privacy and safety decisions with your own data.
